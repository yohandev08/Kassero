# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Primary: Sari-sari store owners, neighborhood micro-retailers, and store cashiers in the Philippines.
- Operating posture: Fast-paced point-of-sale interactions at physical store counters, frequently serving walk-in neighbors and regular community customers.

## Product Purpose
Kassero streamlines daily retail operations for sari-sari stores by unifying rapid point-of-sale checkout, customer credit ("utang") tracking, and inventory management into an accessible, real-time web application. Success means store owners eliminate manual notebook credit ledgers, prevent stockouts, calculate exact change instantly, and gain clear visibility into daily revenues and margins.

## Positioning
Unlike generic POS platforms designed for western retail or enterprise environments, Kassero is tailored for the informal Philippine retail ecosystem where "pautang" (trust-based credit) is central to customer loyalty, digital e-wallet/e-load services are common, and rapid calculations in Philippine Peso (₱) are essential.

## Operating Context
- Countertop transactions on laptops, tablets, or smartphones in fast-paced retail spaces.
- Cash-heavy transactions requiring immediate calculation of tendered amounts and change.
- Informal community relationships where customer debt limits and payment histories must be clear, transparent, and accurate.
- Tracking of mixed payment modes: Cash, Utang (credit), and Digital (GCash/Maya/e-services).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- High-speed counter sales (POS) with real-time cart updates and instant change calculation.
- Utang & customer ledger with credit limit enforcement, partial/full payment recording, and transaction audit trails.
- Inventory tracking with cost vs. selling price margins, stock levels, reorder thresholds, and purchase order drafting.
- Sales & profit analytics with breakdowns across payment types (Cash, Utang, Digital) and top-selling products.
- Currency: Philippine Peso (₱).
- Authentication: Supabase email/password session.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Kassero (Sari-Sari Store Management).
- Tone: Efficient, dependable, clean, and community-friendly.

## Evidence on Hand
- Functional Next.js/React/TypeScript web application in active repository.
- Supabase backend integration for authentication, products, customers, sales, and utang ledger.
- Existing UI components covering POS, Customer Ledger, Inventory Manager, and Sales Analytics.

## Product Principles
1. Fast counter checkout over complex administrative workflows.
2. Clear and transparent utang accounting to preserve neighborhood trust.
3. Unambiguous profit margin visibility so store owners know their actual daily earnings.
4. Mobile and desktop ergonomics that work seamlessly in tight counter spaces.
